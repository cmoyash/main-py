    The project

    Designed around the horizon, not the plot.

    AZURA is a small collection of residences on a limestone headland. Every room was placed by its view first, then its walls. The result is a house that feels like it has nothing in front of it but sea and sky.

    FAQ

    Good questions.

    Can foreign buyers purchase a residence?

    Yes. Our team works with local lawyers who guide international buyers through every step, from reservation to handover.

    What does the reservation process look like?

    After a visit you can reserve with a refundable deposit. A sales contract follows within 30 days, with payments spread across construction milestones.

    When are the residences ready to move into?

    The Cliff Villa and The Pool Pavilion are ready now. The Sky Suite and The Garden House complete in the next construction phase.

    Can I change the interior finishes?

    Residences that are still under construction can choose from three interior palettes, and bespoke changes are possible on request.

    Is there a rental programme?

    Owners can join an optional managed rental programme. The concierge team handles guests, cleaning and maintenance.

    Can I visit virtually?

    Yes. Pick "Video call" as the visit type in the booking form and we will walk you through the residence live.
